# Case studies

These examples show where coding agents helped, what went wrong, and what we learned.

## [An AI Reviewer Found Two Bugs. A Developer Merged Anyway.](/case-studies/30-ai-assisted-commits)

Two useful review comments went unanswered in a PR with 83 conversation items. The case study
shows why an agent needs to make important findings easy to see, explain what happened to
each one, and keep long reports from overwhelming the person reviewing the work.
